Who We Are

A Trusted Name in
Energy Solutions

South West Energy Partners, LLC is a Delaware Limited Liability Company formed on October 8, 2014, headquartered in East Hartford, Connecticut. We specialize in delivering comprehensive energy solutions with a commitment to accuracy, integrity, and long-term value.

With a global vision and a foundation built on strong partnerships, we bridge the gap between energy producers, investors, and international capital markets — ensuring sustainable and compliant transactions across the globe.

Thomas Espy Kelly

Executive Chairman & Founder

NYSE: BPZ NYSE: PUMP Baylor University Permian Basin

Thomas Espy Kelly is a seasoned energy executive and entrepreneur with over four decades of leadership across oil & gas exploration, production, and services. A Baylor University graduate (1977), he co-founded Baytech Inc., growing Permian Basin assets sold to Energen for $206 million. He founded United Fuel & Energy (UFEN), scaling revenues from $100M to over $1 billion before its 2008 sale. He also co-founded Pro Petro Services (NYSE: PUMP) and served as founder & CEO of BPZ Energy (NYSE: BPZ), achieving a $2 billion market cap through major oil discoveries in Peru and Ecuador. Thomas brings unparalleled expertise in energy asset development, international operations, and capital partnerships to South West Energy Partners, LLC.
